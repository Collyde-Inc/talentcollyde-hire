# Examples

This folder ships with the plugin so you can see what the kit produces before running it on your own company.

The example is **Sutter & Vine** — a fictional natural wine subscription, $4.5M ARR, founder Maya Costa hiring a Head of Operations. Fictional on purpose. No real founder data ships with the kit.

The point of the example: show you the two artifacts that demonstrate the moat.

## Look at these first

**`sutter-and-vine/roles/head-of-operations/candidates/jordan-park/scorecard.pdf`** — the marquee artifact. Two pages. The recommendation is **Hold — re-interview**, not Advance, even though the composite passes the minimum. Open the PDF and read why. That outcome is the rubric interrupting the founder's bias on purpose — exactly what Maya asked the kit to do during her intake.

**`sutter-and-vine/roles/head-of-operations/interview_rubric.md`** — read the Observations at the top before the gates and dimensions. Two paragraphs each. They're the kit's response to specific moments in Maya's recorded intake — pointing at what to weight and what to probe.

## What's in the rest of the folder

`.intake/onboarding_transcript.txt` — Maya's recorded founder interview (17 min, Fireflies-style). The raw material the kit synthesized.

`company.md` — the synthesis. Hiring DNA, values, what thrives, what dies. Observations at the top.

`roles/head-of-operations/.intake/role_intake_transcript.txt` — Maya's role intake recording.

`roles/head-of-operations/STATUS.md` — the role's live state.

`candidates/jordan-park/interview_notes.md` — Maya's notes from the screening call. What the scorecard scored against.

## What's not here

The JD, personas, and full interview kit aren't shipped in this example folder. Those get generated when you run `/talentcollyde-hire:intake` on your own role. The folder above shows the two artifacts that prove the kit reads founders like a senior recruiter — the rubric and the scorecard. The rest you'll see when you run it.
