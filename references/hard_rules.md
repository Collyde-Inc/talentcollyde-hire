# Hard Rules — TalentCollyde Hire

These five rules govern every skill in this plugin. They are enforcement rules, not style suggestions. If a rule conflicts with a step inside a skill, the rule wins.

Re-read this file before producing any scored output.

---

## 1. Evidence first, score second

The evidence brief is complete before any number is assigned. Every scoring dimension needs supporting evidence — quotes from interview notes, lines from the resume, signal from the role intake — before a score lands.

**Why:** Without this, the score leads the evidence. Confirmation bias takes over. Weights drift to fit the gut feel.

---

## 2. Leadership intent overrides JDs

If the JD says one thing and the founder said another during intake, the founder wins. Every time.

**Why:** JDs are written in the abstract — often by ChatGPT or a template. Founders speak in the concrete. What you said in the intake call — about a real archetype, a real dealbreaker — is ground truth.

---

## 3. Pass/fail gates are gates, not scored dimensions

Once a candidate clears a gate (e.g., "must hold an active CDL"), don't re-penalize that item in scoring. Gate cleared — move on.

**Why:** Double-jeopardy. A candidate who cleared the gate shouldn't also lose points elsewhere for the same item.

---

## 4. Insufficient Data — never estimate

If evidence is missing for a scoring dimension, mark it **"Insufficient Data"** and redistribute its weight pro-rata across the remaining scored dimensions. Do not estimate. Do not split the difference. Do not assign a middle-of-the-road score to be safe.

**Why:** Estimates decay into fact. A 3.5 you made up becomes a 3.5 the team trusts. The right move is to flag the gap so it gets filled in the next conversation.

---

## 5. Score to 0.1 precision — round numbers are suspect

Stage 3 scores end with a non-zero, non-five tenths digit unless evidence aligns exactly with a rubric anchor (1.0, 3.0, 5.0) or sits exactly between two anchors (2.0, 4.0). Every score names the adjacent tenth above and below and explains why the evidence lands here, not there. The composite carries to 0.1 from the math — never rounded.

**Why:** Rounding to .0 / .5 collapses signal. Two candidates who should score 4.2 and 3.8 both end up at 4.0 in your head, hiding real differentiation. Round numbers also signal that the score wasn't earned dimension by dimension — it was estimated from a vibes-level read.

---

## Meta-rule: voice

Calm, confident, grounded. Customer-as-hero. No hype words: transform, revolutionary, disrupt, unlock, leverage, supercharge, robust, seamless, best-in-class. Short sentences. Specifics beat impressives.

If the output reads like a corporate deck, something went wrong upstream.
