## Weight allocation

The rubric is honest. If `company.md` says integrity is the deepest value but the rubric weighted it 8%, the rubric is lying. Typical shape:

- One dominant dimension at 25–35% (the founder's stated #1 priority)
- 3–4 mid-weight dimensions at 15–20% each
- 1–2 lower-weight dimensions at 5–10% each (still material — not throwaway)

If a dimension can't justify ≥5%, it shouldn't be in the rubric.
