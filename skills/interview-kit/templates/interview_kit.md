## Their questions for you (5 min)

Open the floor: *"What questions do you have for me?"*

**Listen for these patterns:**

- **What they ask first.** If they ask about comp before strategy, that's signal. If they ask about how decisions get made, that's also signal. The first question reveals the priority.
- **Depth.** Surface-level questions ("What's your tech stack?") vs. real ones ("How do you handle disagreement between you and your co-founder?"). Depth shows preparation and judgment.
- **What they don't ask.** If they don't ask about the role's biggest risk, that's a tell. Good candidates probe the hard parts.

## After the interview

1. Within an hour, paste your notes into `roles/<slug>/candidates/<cand-slug>/interview_notes.md`.
2. Run `/talentcollyde-hire:score` — Claude produces the scorecard.
3. Decide.

**Why within an hour:** Memory decays. By tomorrow, you'll round 4.2 candidates up to 4.5 because they were "great," and 3.8 candidates down to 3.5 because they were "fine." Both are wrong. The notes catch the specifics; the rubric catches the score.
